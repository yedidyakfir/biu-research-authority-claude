# ISF expense categories: source research

Source: [Israel Science Foundation personal research grant guidelines (Hebrew, November 2025 application cycle)](https://www.isf.org.il/Files/GuidelinesPersonalGrant_Heb_3058_1.pdf), sections 4 and 6.3.5.

This is a **candidate classification map**, not an eligibility decision. The guide says financial reports follow the approved grant budget lines. Confirm the specific grant track, award year, approved budget, and any written ISF exceptions before using these categories for an official report.

| Budget heading | Hebrew | Examples in the guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personnel | כוח אדם | Research staff and students |
| Research tools and materials | חומרים וכלי מחקר | Consumables and minor research tools |
| Services | שירותים | Consulting, samples or data, participant payments, maintenance, one-time repository deposit |
| Other expenses | הוצאות אחרות | Essential project-specific costs, including narrowly permitted funded student or postdoctoral scientific travel |
| Computers | מחשוב | Software, computers, cloud services, peripherals, dedicated computing |
| Miscellaneous | שונות | Professional literature, journal publication, photocopies, office supplies, office/lab internet, principal investigator society dues |
| Dedicated equipment | ציוד ייעודי | Project-specific equipment |
| Additional funding | מימון תוספתי | Special marine research vessel use |
| Institutional overhead | תקורה | Budget-summary calculation, rather than an ordinary transaction expense |

Key limits in this guide: miscellaneous is capped at 15% of each annual budget; dedicated equipment at ₪120,000 including acquisition costs; qualifying grant-funded graduate student or postdoctoral scientific travel at ₪6,000 per year. General exclusions include principal investigator/applicant and eligible faculty pay, routine travel and per diem, hospitality and foreign visitors, building infrastructure, and warehouse loading above 7% of purchase value. Exceptions require prior written ISF approval.

For each future transaction, retain its original row, proposed heading, explanation, and a review flag when the description, budget line, or eligibility is uncertain.
